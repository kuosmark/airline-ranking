How rankings work

Operators are grouped by their callsign prefix, including cargo, regional and private operators. These are observed counts, not complete worldwide totals. Equal counts are ordered by prefix.

Callsigns matching aircraft registrations are excluded. Unresolved operators appear as codes; callsigns do not guarantee operator identity. Aircraft types use verified labels where available; unfamiliar model labels remain separate.

Arrows compare positions with the previous successful snapshot. NEW means an operator was outside the previous top 100; a dash means no change or no previous comparison. After a delay, the comparison still uses the last successful snapshot.
