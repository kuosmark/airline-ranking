Airline Ranking

Observed airborne aircraft

How does it workRefreshes every 15 minutes

The total includes all fresh, explicitly airborne aircraft observed in SkyLink data; coverage varies. Identifiable operator prefixes are ranked by aircraft count. Operators are grouped by their callsign prefix, including cargo, regional and private operators. These are observed counts, not complete worldwide totals. Equal counts are ordered by prefix.

Aircraft with missing or unrecognized callsigns, including callsigns matching their registration, appear in a separate unranked Unknown operator group. Unresolved operators appear as codes; callsigns do not guarantee operator identity.

Switch to Aircraft types to rank aircraft by ICAO type codes matched against our reference. Their drawers show operators and aircraft locations. Unknown type contains airborne aircraft whose type is missing or could not be matched to a specific ICAO aircraft type. These aircraft remain included in the total and appear on the map when their positions are available. Aircraft type names use a bundled lookup derived from the Mictronics aircraft database, available under the Open Data Commons Attribution License.

Arrows show how many places an operator or aircraft type moved since the previous successful snapshot. NEW means it was not observed in the previous snapshot; a dash means unchanged. A blank movement cell means no previous snapshot is available. After a delay, the comparison still uses the last successful snapshot.
