How does it workRefreshes every 15 minutes

The top 100 operators are ranked by aircraft observed airborne in SkyLink data; coverage varies. Operators are grouped by their callsign prefix, including cargo, regional and private operators. These are observed counts, not complete worldwide totals. Equal counts are ordered by prefix.

Callsigns matching aircraft registrations are excluded. Unresolved operators appear as codes; callsigns do not guarantee operator identity. Aircraft types use verified labels where available; unfamiliar model labels remain separate.

Arrows show how many places an operator moved since the previous successful snapshot. NEW means it was outside the previous top 100; a dash means unchanged. A blank movement cell means no previous snapshot is available. After a delay, the comparison still uses the last successful snapshot.
